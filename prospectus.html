WELCOME TO LIVING PRECIOUS SCHOOLS

At Living Precious Schools (LPS), we believe that every child is unique, gifted and full of possibilities.
Established in January 2007, Living Precious Schools began with a simple but powerful vision: to provide exceptional childcare and meaningful learning experiences that enable children to discover their individual strengths, develop confidence and grow into responsible global citizens.
Located in Lawanson, Surulere, a pleasant residential district in the heart of Lagos, our school provides a warm, stimulating and secure environment for Nigerian and expatriate children from 2 months to 11 years of age.
We are committed to making childhood a pleasant, memorable and enriching experience, while laying a strong foundation for lifelong learning.


OUR VISION

To raise knowledgeable, confident, compassionate and internationally minded children who are equipped to become responsible citizens and creative problem-solvers in an ever-changing world.


OUR MISSION

Our mission is to unlock and develop the unique potential in every child through quality education, excellent childcare, purposeful learning experiences and positive relationships.
We seek to nurture children who are:

  • Caring and considerate
  • Enthusiastic about learning
  • Compassionate towards others
  • Confident and independent
  • Creative and curious
  • Responsible and respectful
  • Open-minded and internationally minded
  • Capable of thinking critically and solving problems

OUR PHILOSOPHY

At Living Precious Schools, we understand that children learn best when they feel safe, valued, loved and encouraged.
We therefore combine quality education with attentive childcare and a child-centred approach to learning. We encourage our pupils to ask questions, explore their environment, express themselves and develop confidence in their abilities.
We believe education should be more than the acquisition of facts. It should help children develop the knowledge, skills, attitudes and character they need to navigate life successfully.
Above all, we want our children to look back on their early years with happy memories of friendship, discovery, achievement and growth.


OUR SCHOOL

Crèche

Our Crèche provides a caring and nurturing environment for babies and toddlers from 2 months.
We understand that entrusting your young child to another person is a significant decision. Our goal is to provide a warm, safe and stimulating environment where children receive attentive care while beginning to explore the world around them. Our early years programme encourages:

  • Sensory exploration
  • Early communication and language development
  • Social interaction
  • Physical development
  • Music, movement and creative activities
  • Early independence and self-help skills

Preschool

Our Preschool programme provides the foundation upon which successful lifelong learning can be built.
Children are encouraged to learn through purposeful play, exploration, interaction and age-appropriate activities. We nurture their curiosity while helping them develop the confidence and foundational skills required for their next stage of education. Areas of development include:

  • Language and communication
  • Early literacy
  • Early numeracy
  • Creative expression
  • Physical development
  • Personal, social and emotional development
  • Environmental awareness
  • Problem-solving and critical thinking

Primary School

Our Primary School caters for children through their primary years, providing a structured and stimulating learning environment designed to support both academic and personal development.
We strive to develop pupils who are confident learners, effective communicators and responsible members of their communities.
Our approach encourages pupils to:

  • Develop strong academic foundations
  • Think independently and creatively
  • Ask questions and investigate
  • Communicate effectively
  • Work collaboratively
  • Solve problems confidently
  • Demonstrate good character and responsibility
  • Appreciate different cultures and perspectives

A CHILD-CENTRED APPROACH

Every child is different.
At LPS, we recognise that children have different personalities, interests, abilities and learning styles. We therefore aim to provide opportunities for every child to discover what they can do and to develop at their own pace while receiving the guidance and encouragement they need.
Our teachers and caregivers work to create positive relationships with our pupils, because we believe that a child who feels valued is better positioned to learn, grow and thrive.

MORE THAN ACADEMICS

We want our pupils to become more than successful students.
We want them to become good people.
Our educational experience therefore places emphasis on character development and values such as:

  • Respect
  • Kindness
  • Honesty
  • Responsibility
  • Compassion
  • Cooperation
  • Confidence
  • Self-discipline

Through everyday interactions and learning experiences, children are encouraged to understand the importance of treating others with dignity and contributing positively to their communities.


PREPARING CHILDREN FOR THE FUTURE

The world our children will grow into is constantly changing.

At Living Precious Schools, we aim to prepare children not only for the next class or examination, but for the opportunities and challenges they will encounter throughout their lives.

We encourage children to become:

  • Curious: Children are encouraged to ask questions and explore new ideas.
  • Creative: We provide opportunities for children to express themselves and develop their imagination.
  • Confident: Children are encouraged to participate, communicate and believe in their abilities.
  • Compassionate: We help children understand the importance of empathy, kindness and consideration for others.
  • Independent: Children are gradually encouraged to take responsibility for themselves, their learning and their environment.
  • Problem Solvers: We nurture the ability to think, reason, investigate and find solutions.
  • Globally Minded: We encourage children to appreciate different people, cultures and perspectives.

OUR PROPRIETRESS

MRS AYOADE IGBOANUGO

Living Precious Schools is the dream and vision of Mrs Ayoade Igboanugo, the school's Proprietress.

An erudite educator and experienced school administrator, Mrs Igboanugo has over 30 years of experience in teaching and school administration in Nigeria.

Her passion for children and education inspired the establishment of Living Precious Schools and continues to shape the school's philosophy.

Her vision is to provide children with an environment where they can receive quality education, develop their individual abilities and enjoy a happy and memorable childhood.

Her commitment to education is reflected in the school's continued focus on nurturing children academically, socially, emotionally and morally.


OUR APPROVAL

Living Precious Schools has received approval from the Lagos State Government to operate its Preschool and Primary School programmes.

Parents can therefore entrust their children's education to a school committed to providing structured learning experiences within an approved educational framework.


WHY CHOOSE LIVING PRECIOUS SCHOOLS?

At LPS, we offer:

  • Experienced educational leadership
  • Over 15 years of school operation since our establishment in 2007
  • Crèche, Preschool and Primary education
  • A warm and child-centred environment
  • Quality childcare and learning experiences
  • Focus on the development of the whole child
  • Emphasis on character and values
  • Support for Nigerian and expatriate families
  • A convenient location in Lawanson, Surulere
  • Lagos State Government approval for our Preschool and Primary School

FOR NIGERIAN AND EXPATRIATE FAMILIES

Whether you are returning to work, relocating to Lagos from another part of Nigeria, moving back from abroad, or simply looking for a nurturing and stimulating school for your child, Living Precious Schools welcomes you.

We understand that families have different needs and that choosing the right school requires careful consideration.

We invite you to visit us, meet our team, experience our environment and discover how we can support your child's educational journey.
