Health Awareness Programme Promotes Student Well-being

The school organized a comprehensive health awareness programme aimed at educating students on healthy lifestyles, personal hygiene, nutrition, and disease prevention.

Healthcare professionals shared practical advice while interactive demonstrations encouraged students to adopt healthy daily habits.

Living Precious Schools remains committed to nurturing healthy minds and healthy bodies.

Living Precious Schools opened its doors in January 2007 to operate as a Crèche, Preschool and Primary. We are situated in Surulere a pleasant residential district in the heart of Lagos, Nigeria. We provide wonderful childcare and learning experiences for Nigerian and expatriate children between the ages of 2 months to 11 years. Our aim is to assist them in developing into caring, enthusiastic, compassionate and internationally minded problem solvers and learners.
